Why We Started

Mini splits are one of the most efficient ways to heat and cool a home — but the retail markup on these systems has always been difficult to justify. The units are the same. The quality is the same. The price shouldn’t be.

Mini Split Depot was built on a simple idea: source directly from MRCOOL as an authorized partner and pass those savings to you. We operate lean, ship fast, and price every unit competitively — no sales, no gimmicks, no fine print.

Whether you’re cooling a single bedroom or an entire floor, we carry the size you need at a price that makes sense.
