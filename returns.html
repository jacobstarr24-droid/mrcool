Return Eligibility

We accept returns on orders that meet all of the following conditions:

  • The return is requested within 30 days of the delivery date
  • The unit has not been installed (lineset connections not made, unit not mounted)
  • The unit is in its original packaging in undamaged, resaleable condition
  • All original components and accessories are included

How to Start a Return

To initiate a return, contact us with the following information:

  • Your order number
  • The reason for the return
  • Photos of the unit and packaging if applicable

We will respond within 24 hours with return shipping instructions. Do not ship any items back before receiving return authorization from us.

Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned unit, we will process your refund within 5–7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund has been processed.

Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, defective unit, or damage that occurred during transit).

Installed Units

Units that have been installed cannot be returned. Installation voids return eligibility because the refrigerant line connections and mounting process cannot be undone without affecting the unit’s condition and warranty registration. If you have an issue with an installed unit, please contact us and we will help you work through the manufacturer warranty process.

Damaged or Defective Units

If your unit arrived damaged or is defective out of the box, contact us immediately. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you, including return shipping. Please document any damage with photos at the time of delivery.

Warranty Claims

All units carry the manufacturer’s warranty (7-year compressor, 5-year parts when registered within 30 days). Warranty claims for installed units are handled directly through the manufacturer. We are happy to assist you initiate a warranty claim — just reach out and we’ll point you in the right direction.

Questions?

If you have any questions about returns or refunds, please contact us. We respond within 24 hours, Mon – Fri.
