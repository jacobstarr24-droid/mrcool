Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Mini Split Depot (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide to us through our contact form, including:

  • Your name and email address
  • The subject and content of your message
  • Any additional information you choose to include

We do not collect payment information or browsing history. We do not use cookies or analytics tracking scripts. However, this site loads fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), which may transmit your IP address to Google as part of that request. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details on how they handle that data.

Our cart and order system uses your browser’s localStorage to store your cart contents and order history locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Contact Form Processing

Our contact form is processed by Web3Forms, a third-party service that forwards your message to our email address. Your submission data is processed according to Web3Forms’ privacy policy. We do not store form submissions on our servers.

Cookies & Local Storage

This website does not use cookies or analytics tracking scripts. We use browser localStorage solely to maintain your shopping cart and local order history — this data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us. The Google Fonts CDN used to load typefaces on this site may set its own cookies per Google’s policies.

Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to MRCOOL’s website and other third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review their privacy policies independently.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide to us. However, no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our contact form.
