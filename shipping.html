Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about how and when your order ships. Last updated: May 2026

Free Shipping On all orders to the contiguous US
1–2 Business Days Processing time
5–7 Business Days Estimated delivery

Free Shipping

We offer free standard shipping on all orders shipped to addresses in the contiguous United States. Shipping to Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories may be subject to additional fees — contact us before ordering if you need delivery outside the contiguous US.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days of placement. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will begin processing on the next business day.

Delivery Estimates

Once shipped, standard delivery takes approximately 5–7 business days depending on your location. Delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed. Remote or rural addresses may take slightly longer.

Order Tracking

You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number once your order has been dispatched. You can use this tracking number on the carrier’s website to monitor your shipment in real time.

If you have not received a tracking number within 2 business days of placing your order, please contact us and we will look into it right away.

Packaging

Mini split units are large, heavy items and ship via freight or LTL (less-than-truckload) carrier. The delivery will typically be curbside — the carrier will bring the shipment to the curb or driveway. If you require inside delivery or white-glove service, please contact us prior to ordering.

Damaged Shipments

Please inspect your shipment carefully at delivery before signing. If you notice visible damage to the packaging or the unit, note the damage on the delivery receipt and take photos before accepting. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at our contact page and we will work to resolve the issue promptly.

Expedited Shipping

Expedited shipping may be available on a case-by-case basis. Contact us before placing your order to inquire about faster delivery options and any associated costs.

Questions?

If you have any questions about shipping, don’t hesitate to reach out. We typically respond within 24 hours, Mon – Fri.
